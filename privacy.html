AIOS - Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

1. Overview

AIOS is a personal desktop AI assistant designed to help users manage creative workflows and their own social media content.

2. Information Accessed

When a user connects a third-party service such as TikTok, AIOS may access information authorized by that user through the permissions granted during the authentication process.

Depending on the permissions granted, this may include basic account information and information about the user's own published content.

3. How Information Is Used

Information obtained from connected services is used only to provide features explicitly requested by the user, such as displaying and analyzing their own social media content.

4. Authentication Data

Authentication credentials and access tokens are used only to communicate with services authorized by the user. AIOS does not sell authentication data or social media account information.

5. Third-Party Services

AIOS may interact with third-party platforms such as TikTok. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Revoking Access

Users may revoke AIOS access to a connected third-party account through the relevant platform's account or authorization settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated as AIOS evolves or as its integrations with third-party services change.

8. Contact

Questions regarding this privacy policy can be submitted through the AIOS project owner.
